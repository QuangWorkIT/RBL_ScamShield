\section{INTRODUCTION}
\label{sec:intro}

Telecommunications fraud, smishing (SMS phishing), and brand impersonation inflict severe financial and social damage across Southeast Asia, with estimated fraud losses in Vietnam exceeding 18.9 trillion VND (US \$744 million) in 2024~\cite{vietnamnet2024scams}, \cite{mst2024scams}. Threat actors systematically exploit localized linguistic evasion maneuvers, including regional internet slang, teencode abbreviations, non-standard punctuation, and diacritic-stripped text, to circumvent carrier-level regex filters and static keyword blacklists~\cite{seo2024ondevice}.

While deep transformer encoders~\cite{nguyen2020phobert}, \cite{nguyen2023visobert} and Large Language Models (LLMs)~\cite{saias2025advances} offer advanced contextual representation, existing telecommunications defenses encounter three operational barriers:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Symmetric Loss Misalignment:} Conventional deep learning classifiers are trained under symmetric cross-entropy loss. In fraud defense, error costs are highly asymmetric: misclassifying a benign message incurs transient annoyance, whereas missing a scam (False Negative) causes financial extortion. Symmetric loss biases models toward the majority benign class, suppressing recall on evasive lures.
    \item \textbf{Solitary LLM Hyper-Vigilance:} Cloud LLMs deployed as solitary zero- or few-shot detectors exhibit severe alarm fatigue. Over-sensitized to urgency cues and hyperlinks in legitimate alerts (e.g., banking OTPs), solitary models suffer precision collapse, exhibiting an empirical False Discovery Rate of $35.34\%$ ($41/116$) and False Positive Rate of $21.35\%$ ($41/192$) on authentic messages in our frozen test partition.
    \item \textbf{Edge Latency and Privacy Constraints:} Querying cloud LLMs introduces multi-second latency bottlenecks ($3\text{--}15\text{ s}$) and recurring API fees, while transmitting raw personal SMS off-device introduces privacy risks.
\end{enumerate}

To address these challenges, this study presents an empirical evaluation of \textbf{ScamShield-VN}, a two-tier edge--cloud triage architecture combining a lightweight on-device screener with an Occam-consolidated three-agent forensic council. Rather than proposing a new foundational learning algorithm, this work provides a rigorous applied evaluation of cost-sensitive edge fine-tuning, confidence-based deferral, and adversarial multi-agent deliberation tailored to Vietnamese telecommunications fraud. We investigate three core Research Questions (RQs):
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{RQ1 (Edge Screener Efficiency):} Can ViSoBERT fine-tuned under Cost-Sensitive Weighted Binary Cross-Entropy ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{WBCE}}$, $\alpha = 5.0$) and quantized via ONNX INT8 achieve $\ge 95\%$ scam recall and significantly outperform solitary cloud LLMs in paired correctness while operating under edge CPU latency constraints?
    \item \textbf{RQ2 (Council Deliberation and False-Alarm Suppression):} Does a 3-agent forensic council with an adversarial Public Defender suppress false alarms on authentic notifications ($0.00\%$ target on banking OTPs) compared to single-LLM baselines, and does it maintain paired decision consistency with 5-agent panels?
    \item \textbf{RQ3 (System Trade-offs and Operating Viability):} What Pareto trade-offs emerge across edge resolution fraction, end-to-end latency, and API expenditure when evaluating the cascaded two-tier architecture across routing confidence thresholds $\tau \in [0.70, 0.95]$?
\end{itemize}

Our primary empirical contributions are: (1) An empirical benchmark of $2,664$ real-world Vietnamese telecommunications messages ($N=267$ locked frozen holdout) evaluating transformer backbones against cloud LLMs; (2) A quantized edge screener ($97.57\text{M}$ params, $98.42\text{ MB}$ / $93.86\text{ MiB}$, $70.7\text{ ms}$ CPU latency) achieving $96.00\%$ holdout recall ($72/75$) and $4.17\%$ FPR ($8/192$), significantly surpassing Gemini 3.5 Flash ($p < 0.0001$, McNemar test); and (3) A two-tier cascade resolving $96.6\%$ of traffic locally, achieving $97.40\%$ overall accuracy, $2.37\%$ FPR, and an estimated marginal API cost of $\$0.0153$ per 1,000 messages with $267.5\text{ ms}$ mean system latency.
