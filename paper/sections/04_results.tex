\subsection{Discussion of Scientific Pillars}

\subsubsection{Correlated Error Mitigation and Rejection of Condorcet Jury Theorem}
A central conceptual hazard in multi-agent LLM systems is the inappropriate application of the Condorcet Jury Theorem. Condorcet's theorem guarantees that majority voting among $m$ agents converges to an error rate of zero as $m \to \infty$, under the strict assumption that individual voter decisions are statistically independent: $P(E_i \cap E_j) = P(E_i)P(E_j)$. 

However, recent empirical findings by Kim et al.~\cite{kim2025correlated} and Smit et al.~\cite{smit2024should} demonstrated that foundation LLMs share overlapping training corpora, inducing strong correlated failure modes ($>60\%$ co-failure on deceptive adversarial inputs). Under correlated error distributions, Ladha~\cite{ladha1992condorcet} proved that increasing agent count fails to guarantee asymptotic convergence and can amplify collective bias. This theoretical limitation explains why naive majority voting (Arm 4 in Table~\ref{tab:e2e_benchmark}) yielded a higher FPR ($2.84\%$) than our proposed architecture. ScamShield-VN overcomes correlated failure not through homogeneous voting, but through deliberate adversarial role specialization: Seat C (Public Defender) is explicitly incentivized to seek counter-evidence against threat hypotheses, breaking error correlation on legitimate transactional messages.

\subsubsection{Neutralizing the Alarm Fatigue Phenomenon}
Commercial LLMs operating as solitary screeners exhibit catastrophic false-alarm rates ($35.53\%$ FAR on Gemini 3.5 Flash) due to hyper-vigilance toward urgency markers and URLs. In human-factors security, chronic false alarms induce \textit{alarm fatigue}, conditioning mobile users to distrust or disable security applications. The introduction of the Public Defender's $-0.25$ veto completely neutralized this failure mode, achieving $0.00\%$ FPR on authentic banking OTPs in the forensic challenge suite.

\subsubsection{Failure of Parameter Scaling Without Asymmetric Loss}
Our benchmarks demonstrate that increasing model parameters cannot substitute for an asymmetric loss formulation. PhoBERT-large contains 370M parameters ($3.4\times$ larger than ViSoBERT) and required $3.45\times$ more training time ($1,457.7\text{ s}$ vs. $422.2\text{ s}$), yet missed $3.29\%$ more scams ($92.33\%$ vs. $95.62\%$). ViSoBERT's domain-specific pre-training on 60 GB of Vietnamese social media, combined with $\mathcal{L}_{\text{WBCE}}$ ($\alpha=5.0$), proved far more impactful than raw parameter scaling.

\subsubsection{Data Privacy and Safety-First Verdict Protocol}
In compliance with Article 25 of Vietnamese Decree 13/2023/ND-CP, ScamShield-VN rejects forced binary classification on borderline samples. By providing an explicit \textbf{UNKNOWN} label for ambiguous samples ($0.40 \le P < 0.60$), the platform preserves human-in-the-loop auditability. Furthermore, client-side PII redaction ensures subscriber privacy before cloud escalation.


\subsection{Threats to Validity}
\textbf{Internal Validity:} Random weight initialization and non-deterministic training convergence can induce performance variance. To control for this, all neural models were benchmarked over $K = 5$ fixed deterministic seeds ($\text{SEEDS} = [42, 100, 123, 999, 2026]$) reporting exact means and standard deviations. Cloud LLM queries were executed at temperature 0.0 to guarantee deterministic greedy decoding.

\textbf{External Validity:} Our empirical benchmark explicitly evaluates Vietnamese telecommunications text. While localized to Vietnamese social engineering lures, the two-tier routing framework and the Public Defender de-correlation architecture are structurally agnostic and transferrable to other low-resource regional languages.

\textbf{Construct Validity:} Data leakage was rigorously prevented via strict deduplication and stratified isolation of the $N = 267$ holdout partition prior to any hyperparameter search or fine-tuning. Ground-truth annotations were validated with high inter-annotator agreement (Cohen's $\kappa = 0.88$).

\textbf{Conclusion Validity:} To avoid reliance on uncalibrated point estimates, paired classification discordances were formally evaluated using continuity-corrected two-tailed McNemar's tests ($\alpha = 0.05$), ensuring that empirical claims are supported by rigorous statistical hypothesis testing.
