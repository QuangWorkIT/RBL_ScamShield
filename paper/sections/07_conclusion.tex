\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This study evaluated the performance of real-time, on-device scam detection for Vietnamese telecommunications messages. The results show that fine-tuning ViSoBERT with Cost-Sensitive Weighted Binary Cross-Entropy loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{WBCE}}$, $\alpha = 5.0$) achieved a Scam Recall of $95.62\%\pm 2.25\%$ and a Macro-F1 score of $92.51\% \pm 2.39\%$ on a fixed benchmark partition of $N = 267$ samples. ViSoBERT also significantly outperformed few-shot Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite ($p < 0.0001$, McNemar test). Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite achieved a precision of $64.47\%$, indicating a greater risk of false-positive predictions.

These results suggest that combining Vietnamese social-media pre-training with cost-sensitive learning can improve scam detection while supporting on-device deployment. The fine-tuned model achieved an inference time of $15,\text{ms/message}$ with an ONNX model footprint of $390,\text{MB}$. Compared with the unweighted models evaluated in this study, the proposed approach also offers a smaller model footprint. However, the current implementation analyzes plain-text SMS messages only. It does not yet support QR-code analysis or verification of URLs embedded in messages.

Future work will focus on three areas. First, quantized vision-language models will be investigated for on-device QR-code phishing detection. Second, pilot deployments within Vietnamese telecommunications networks will be conducted to evaluate the system under real-world conditions. Third, the proposed 5-Agent Multi-LLM Forensic Council will be evaluated on live telecommunications streams to examine how its agents reach consensus and how their combined decisions affect detection performance.
