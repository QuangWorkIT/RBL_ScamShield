\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}

% --- ĐOẠN 1: TÓM TẮT TRẢ LỜI RQ ---
This paper presented an empirical investigation into real-time on-device scam detection for Vietnamese telecommunications communications. Addressing our core research question, our results demonstrate that fine-tuning ViSoBERT under a Cost-Sensitive Weighted Binary Cross-Entropy loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{WBCE}}$, $\alpha=5.0$) achieves a dominant Scam Recall of $95.62\% \pm 2.25\%$ and Macro-F1 of $92.51\% \pm 2.39\%$ on a frozen $N=267$ benchmark partition. ViSoBERT significantly outperformed few-shot Gemini 3.5 Flash ($p < 0.0001$, McNemar test), which suffered severe alarm fatigue with precision collapsing to $64.47\%$.

% --- ĐOẠN 2: ĐÓNG GÓP CỐT LÕI & GIỚI HẠN CHÍNH ---
Our primary contribution is the demonstration that domain-specific social pre-training combined with cost-asymmetric penalty weighting achieves Pareto-optimal edge detection ($15\text{ ms/msg}$, $390\text{ MB}$ ONNX footprint), surpassing unweighted models that are nearly $4\times$ larger. A key limitation of our current implementation is that on-device triage is restricted to plain-text SMS semantics, without multimodal verification of embedded QR codes or live URL endpoints.

% --- ĐOẠN 3: CÁC HƯỚNG FUTURE WORK KHẢ THI ---
Future work will expand in three promising directions: (1) integrating quantized vision-language models for edge-based QR phishing verification; (2) conducting real-world pilot deployments within Vietnamese carrier networks; and (3) empirically benchmarking the complete consensus dynamics of the 5-Agent Multi-LLM Forensic Council on live telecommunications streams.
