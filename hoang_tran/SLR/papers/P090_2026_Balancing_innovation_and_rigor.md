## Abstract
How can we realize the potential of large language models (LLMs) to enhance evaluations while maintaining rigor? This chapter provides good practices for integrating LLM into evaluation workflows based on our experiments with a frequently occurring use case: identification of relevant literature for inclusion in structured literature reviews. Our experiments’ results reveal that LLM performed well on text classification to identify relevant research papers, achieving satisfactory accuracy. We found iterative prompt validation and refinement, measurement of model performance with relevant metrics, and representative sampling to be important considerations to ensure the success of these applications.
